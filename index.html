The framework

Three phases, one path back

Running too early is the biggest risk to a full recovery. I built KneeRun to pace you through the phases in the order your knee actually needs them.

01 · Recover

Rebuild the foundation

Range of motion, swelling control, and quad activation, tracked daily so you know you're actually ready for what's next, not just guessing.

02 · Rebuild

Earn your strength back

Structured strength and single-leg work, benchmarked against real return-to-run criteria like quad symmetry, not an arbitrary calendar date.

03 · Run

Return with confidence

A gradual run progression that respects your graft, your data, and your nerves. Built to stop you going too far, too fast.
